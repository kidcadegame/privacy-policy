Privacy Policy

Last Updated: May 16, 2026

Introduction

Welcome to Kidcade. Your privacy is important to us. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, protect, and manage information when you use our mobile application.

By using the Kidcade app, you agree to the practices described in this Privacy Policy.

Our goal is to provide a safe, enjoyable, and family-friendly gaming experience for children and families.

Information We Collect

We collect only the minimum information necessary to provide app functionality and improve user experience.

  • Name (when signing in with Google)
  • Email address (when signing in with Google)
  • Profile picture
  • Gameplay progress
  • App preferences and settings
We do not collect sensitive personal information from users or children.

Google Sign-In

Kidcade may allow users to sign in using their Google account.

If you choose to use Google Sign-In, we may access:

  • Your public Google profile name
  • Email address
  • Profile image

This information is used only for:

  • Showing your profile inside the app
  • Providing personalized gameplay experience
  • Saving user-related game data
We never access your Google password.
Google authentication features are under development and may not be available in the current version of the application.

Local Storage

Some game-related information is stored locally on your device for a better user experience.

  • High scores
  • Completed levels
  • Reward points
  • Sound settings
  • Theme preferences

This information remains on your device unless the app is removed or app data is cleared.

Information We Do NOT Collect

Kidcade respects user privacy and does not collect unnecessary data.

We do NOT collect:

• Location information
• Contacts
• SMS or call logs
• Camera recordings
• Microphone recordings
• Financial or payment information

Children's Privacy

Kidcade is designed for kids and families.

We are committed to protecting children's privacy and complying with applicable child privacy regulations.

We do not knowingly collect sensitive personal information from children.

Parents or guardians may contact us if they believe a child has provided personal information through the app.

Third-Party Services

Our application may use trusted third-party services to provide app functionality.

  • Google Sign-In
  • Firebase Authentication
  • Firebase Analytics
  • Firebase Cloud Services

These services may process limited technical information according to their own privacy policies.

Please review Google’s privacy policy for more information.

Advertising

Kidcade currently does not sell personal information to advertisers.

We do not use personalized advertising based on user personal data.

Data Security

We take reasonable measures to protect user information and prevent unauthorized access.

While we strive to use commercially acceptable methods to protect data, no method of electronic storage or transmission over the internet is completely secure.

Changes to This Privacy Policy

Any changes will be posted on this page with an updated revision date.

Continued use of the app after changes means you accept the updated Privacy Policy.
